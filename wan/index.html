Pixel-space in-betweening

Two keys in. A video model fills the gap, as pixels.

Our own run of Wan 2.2 keyframe-to-video between two keys of a boxer. It starts on the first key and lands on the second, and in between it throws punches nobody drew.

It's smooth, but it's a picture on the model's own timing: a studio can take the clip or redraw it. Rabbit Labs draws the in-betweens as editable lines, on the animator's timing, so the artist fixes one line and keeps the rest.
