How it works

  1. Import keys

    Vector line art (SVG or Grease Pencil) plus your exposure sheet. Holds and timing stay yours.

  2. Generate

    Our model matches lines between keys, moves them along the motion and hides lines that pass behind others.

  3. Review

    Scrub with onion skin, accept drawing by drawing, undo anything. Locked drawings are never touched.

Measured, not promised

~2 pxaverage error on held-out character animation. Simply repeating the key is off by 10–16 px.
<1 sper in-between, on a laptop.
100%vector. Every line stays editable in your pipeline.
