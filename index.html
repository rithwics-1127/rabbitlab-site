Why

One second of animation is up to 24 drawings. Almost every one is still drawn by hand.

artist-hours to draw one 22-minute TV episode.

4 for 3

Our base case: give each animator 10 hours a week back and the same team makes four episodes in the time of three.

How it works

01

Drop in keys

Your key drawings as vector line art (SVG or Grease Pencil), with your exposure sheet. Holds and timing stay yours.

02

Generate

Our model matches each line between the keys and moves it along the motion, on the frames your timing calls for.

03

Fix

Point at a wrong line and pick a retake. Only that line is redrawn. Lock anything you've fixed by hand.

What stays yours

Your timing. Your lines. Your building.

We draw on the frames your sheet asks for. Output is vector, so nobody redraws a frame for a note. The model runs on your own machines, and your art is never used for training without your permission.
